01 / Candy Castle

Candy Castle Children Story

A sweet, imaginative world where fantasy meets everyday curiosity

Candy Castle This vibrant sequence tells a playful children's story set in a candy-colored town where a magical ice cream house appears. Each scene bursts with saturated colors, soft textures, and whimsical characters. The narrative unfolds with excited townspeople — from curious kids to surprised adults — interacting with the edible structure. The illustrations blend humor and exaggeration in a stylized, almost theatrical way, using a consistent pink-toned palette and dynamic compositions to guide the viewer through the joyful chaos. It's a sweet, imaginative world where fantasy meets everyday curiosity.

02 / Graphic Novel

Graphic Novel

Urban solitude and the poetry of everyday spaces.

Graphic Novel A short graphic story about one determined dog, a closed door, and the urgent mystery of midnight needs. Armed with nothing but paws, persistence, and a bit of dramatic flair, our furry hero takes on the ultimate household challenge: waking a human. Will the mission succeed? Let's just say… no door stands a chance when nature calls.

03 / Matilde

Matilde

A portrait study in character and emotion.

Matilde A symbolic portrait of my sister. This piece blends abstract textures with narrative light. The golden beam cutting through the cloudy sky suggests clarity and purpose, illuminating a quiet field where a small golden house rests among stylized trees. It reflects her introspective strength. A contemporary polymath who, despite her vast ambitions, sometimes dreams only of a simple garden. The layered digital brushwork and grainy overlays create a tactile and atmospheric landscape that feels both cosmic and deeply person

04 / Beach

Beach

Where ocean meets memory and dreams take flight.

Beach This beach scene uses a bold, minimalist style with clean vector shapes and vibrant color blocks. The composition is stripped down to essentials, creating a graphic, almost abstract feel. The flat tones, exaggerated shadows, and geometric forms give the illustration a retro yet modern aesthetic, while the figure's relaxed pose and melting ground suggest a playful twist on summer heat and stillness. It's a visual study in simplicity, contrast, and stylized form.

05 / Walk

Walk

Journey through sun-drenched paths and golden moments.

Walk This piece captures a quiet moment of togetherness, as two figures walk barefoot under the warm sun. The painterly textures and layered brushstrokes give the landscape a soft, tactile quality, blending digital techniques with a natural, almost hand-printed feel. Bold color blocks and visible marks evoke movement in the trees and ground, while the lighting guides the eye gently into the horizon. It's a peaceful and intimate scene that balances detail with abstraction.

06 / Fashion illustrations

Fashion illustrations

Electric blue and intense red

Fashion illustrations These fashion illustrations emphasize bold color, elongated proportions, and expressive poses to capture both style and attitude. The textured digital brushwork adds depth to the flat color fields, creating a balance between graphic design and painterly detail. The use of saturated backgrounds — electric blue and intense red — frames each figure with striking contrast, enhancing the drama and elegance of their silhouettes. It's a contemporary, stylized approach that celebrates both the body and the garment with visual confidence.

07 / Jungle

Jungle

Lost in the embrace of wild, untamed nature.

Jungle immerses viewers in the dense, mysterious world of tropical wilderness. This piece explores the relationship between human presence and the overwhelming power of nature. The rich, layered composition and deep greens create an atmosphere of both wonder and reverence for the natural world, celebrating the complexity and beauty of ecosystems beyond human control.

08 / Breakfast

Breakfast

Tender moments shared over morning rituals.

Breakfast captures the intimate warmth of shared morning moments between an elderly couple. This piece explores the quiet beauty of everyday rituals, where simple acts of care and companionship create profound connections. The soft lighting and gentle composition reflect the peaceful contentment found in life's simple pleasures and enduring love.

09 / Sleeping

Sleeping

Finding peace in moments of rest and solitude.

Sleeping A student sleeps at her desk, oblivious to the teacher and classmates behind her. The bold lines, comic-inspired halftones, and vibrant purples create a dramatic yet dreamy atmosphere. The mix of hand-drawn textures with digital shading gives the piece a dynamic, layered feel, capturing the tension between daydreaming and the structured world of the classroom.

10 / Self-Portrait

Self-Portrait

Flattened shapes and a limited red-black-white palette

Self-Portrait This bold self-portrait uses flattened shapes and a limited red-black-white palette to explore identity and duality. Overlapping features create tension and balance, suggesting multiple perspectives within a single expression. The style is graphic and symbolic, relying on clean vector forms and strong contrasts to express inner complexity with striking simplicity.
