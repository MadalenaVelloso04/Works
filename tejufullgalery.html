TEJU, Making Off Glam: An Inclusive Beauty Revolution

A collaborative project with INKLU and Vogue Portugal, creating a shower gel that celebrates authenticity, inclusivity, and the beauty of being natural — no glam required.
First semester of university, 2022

Adobe Photoshop Adobe Illustrator Blender AutoCAD
01 / Overview

The Challenge

How do you create a shower gel that champions authenticity in a world obsessed with perfection?

We collaborated with marketing agency INKLU to develop a shower gel centered around the theme of "making off glam." Our goal was to emphasize transparency, inclusivity, and naturality throughout the design and concept.

This shower gel represented a partnership between INKLU, us, and Vogue Portugal, combining our shared vision for a product that celebrates authentic beauty and embraces diverse identities. Inclusivity became the heartbeat of our philosophy.

"Making off glam - celebrating beauty without makeup"
Teju Brand Philosophy
02 / Concept & Creation

Design Philosophy

The cap design embodied our slogan, "making off glam" — symbolizing the moment of removing a smooth-surfaced cap to reveal the imperfect texture underneath.

Core Values

We celebrated all genders and championed the idea of beauty without makeup, recognizing and valuing the rich diversity of personalities and tastes that exist in the world.

Unique Characteristics

The textural detail of the bottle added depth and character to our product. The curved cut-out on the cap distinguished our shower gel from conventional designs, transforming it into not just a product, but a memorable experience.

04 / Packaging Design

Technical Specifications

Functional design meets sustainable manufacturing requirements.

Design Requirements

The packaging design requires a 200 ml container with a maximum height of 150 mm. The container body should be shaped for extrusion blow molding, while the cap will be produced using injection-molded polypropylene (PP).

Stability for upright display and space for labeling are essential for branding and product information. These requirements ensure a compact, practical, and visually cohesive design suitable for production and user-friendly use.
