Fashion Academy Brand Identity

A modern, editorial, and avant-garde brand identity for a fashion academy. The concept explores "an eye for fashion" through sophisticated visual language that combines vision and style in contemporary design.

Challenge
Developing an identity for a fashion academy that positions itself as modern, editorial, and avant-garde. Creating a visual system that captures the essence of vision and fashion expertise.
Solution
Logo concept inspired by "an eye for fashion" where the O becomes an eye. Two O's with a small head form a figure in a flowing dress, creating sophisticated symbolism.
