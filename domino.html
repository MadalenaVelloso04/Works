ColorADD Domino: Inclusive Game Design for Color Blind Players

A professional collaboration with ColorADD to reinvent the classic domino game, making it accessible for color blind players through innovative visual symbols and inclusive design principles.

Game Design Accessibility ColorADD System Inclusive Design
01 / Overview

The Challenge

ColorADD is a revolutionary company that developed a colorblind alphabet system to make the world more accessible. After successfully implementing their system in games like UNO, they partnered with us to reinvent the classic domino game for color blind players.

This professional project gave us the incredible opportunity to work directly with Miguel Neiva, the founder of ColorADD who is also colorblind himself. His personal experience and vision guided our design process as we reimagined how dominoes could be played by everyone, regardless of color vision.

The ColorADD system uses simple, intuitive symbols that represent colors, creating a universal language that makes color-coded games accessible to the 350 million people worldwide who experience color blindness. Our challenge was to integrate this system seamlessly into the domino game while maintaining the classic gameplay experience.

"Making the world more accessible, one game at a time."
Miguel Neiva, ColorADD Founder
02 / Design System

ColorADD Implementation

The ColorADD system transforms traditional color-based games into inclusive experiences through a carefully designed symbol alphabet that represents different colors and their combinations.

Symbol System

Each domino piece features both the traditional colored dots and the corresponding ColorADD symbols. This dual approach allows color blind players to participate fully while maintaining the familiar visual experience for all players. The symbols are strategically placed to be easily readable without disrupting the classic domino aesthetic.

Game Mechanics

The game maintains all traditional domino rules while adding the ColorADD symbols as the primary identification method. Players can now match pieces based on symbols rather than relying solely on color recognition, opening up the game to a much wider audience.

03 / Process

Design Journey

Working directly with Miguel Neiva, the founder of ColorADD, was an incredible experience. Having the opportunity to collaborate with someone who not only created this revolutionary system but also experiences color blindness firsthand brought invaluable insights to our design process.

This professional project challenged us to think beyond traditional game design and consider accessibility as a core design principle rather than an afterthought. Miguel's guidance helped us understand the real-world impact of inclusive design and how small changes can open up entire experiences to new audiences.

The project deepened our understanding of universal design principles and showed us how innovation can come from addressing real human needs. Seeing the ColorADD system in action and working to implement it in dominoes was both educational and inspiring.

01

Research & Understanding

Learning about color blindness and the ColorADD system through direct collaboration with Miguel Neiva. Understanding the challenges faced by color blind players and how the symbol system addresses these barriers.

02

Symbol Integration

Developing methods to seamlessly integrate ColorADD symbols into traditional domino design. Testing various placement options and sizes to ensure readability while maintaining aesthetic appeal.

03

Game Testing

Prototyping and testing the new domino design with both color blind and non-color blind players. Iterating based on feedback to ensure the game remained intuitive and enjoyable for all players.

04

Final Implementation

Refining the final design and creating comprehensive game instructions that incorporate both traditional color-based and symbol-based gameplay explanations.

The project taught us that inclusive design isn't just about accommodation—it's about creating better experiences for everyone. The ColorADD symbols actually made the game easier to play in low-light conditions and helped all players identify pieces more quickly.

This collaboration with ColorADD and Miguel Neiva opened our eyes to the power of design to create meaningful change. It reinforced our belief that good design should be accessible to everyone, not just a privileged few.
