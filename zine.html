Body Bloom: Movement as Nature

Sixth semester of university, 2025

Adobe Illustrator Adobe InDesign Brand Identity
01 / Overview

The Concept

Body Bloom was one of my last personal projects, it's based on my own passion, ballet, which has been a big part of my life. This zine was based of image manipulation, combining multiple ones in a seemless way.

02 / Narrative

The visual Narrative

As a former dancer I loved this personal project where I could express what I've always felt when dance: being one with nature. The b&w texturized treatment creates a poetic language that I meticulously played with.
