K-AFE

A Contemporary Café Experience

A warm, minimalistic brand identity inspired by Korean café culture, featuring hand-drawn mascot coffee cups that embody comfort, coziness, and personal connection through thoughtful design and authentic storytelling.

Challenge
Creating a brand identity rooted in Korean café culture, emphasizing warmth and personal connection while maintaining a contemporary, minimalist aesthetic that feels both authentic and universally welcoming.
Solution
Hand-drawn mascot system with minimalist design principles. Three distinct coffee cup characters create emotional connection while clean layouts and warm colors ensure sophisticated approachability.
