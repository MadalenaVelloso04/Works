K-AFE: Embracing Korean Café Comfort

A warm, minimalistic brand identity inspired by Korean café culture, featuring hand-drawn mascot coffee cups that embody comfort, coziness, and personal connection.
Personal Project, 2023

Adobe Photoshop Adobe Illustrator
01 / Overview

The Challenge

This creative exercise explored developing a brand identity rooted in Korean café culture, emphasizing the warm, personal, and comforting aspects that make these spaces so beloved.

The challenge was to create a visual system that captures the essence of Korean café culture—known for its cozy atmosphere, attention to detail, and emphasis on creating a "third place" between home and work. The goal was to develop something that feels both authentically Korean and universally welcoming.

Our approach centered on minimalism and warmth, drawing inspiration from the clean, uncluttered aesthetic of Korean design while introducing playful elements that make the brand feel personal and approachable. The focus was on creating an identity that would make customers feel cared for and comfortable.

"Comfort is found in the simple moments—a warm cup, a gentle smile, a space that feels like home."
K-AFE Brand Philosophy
02 / Visual Identity

Brand DNA

The visual identity embraces minimalism and warmth, creating a gentle and approachable aesthetic that reflects the comforting nature of Korean café culture.

Hand-Drawn Mascot System

The heart of the brand is a trio of hand-drawn coffee cup mascots, each with distinct personality traits that represent different aspects of the café experience. These characters bring humanity and playfulness to the brand, creating an emotional connection with customers. The hand-drawn quality adds authenticity and warmth, making the brand feel personal and caring rather than corporate.

Typography & Logo

The K-AFE logo is hand-drawn to maintain the wonky, human feel that runs throughout the brand. This intentional imperfection creates authenticity and makes the brand feel approachable and genuine. The typography system balances clean, readable fonts with the playful hand-drawn elements.

Color Philosophy

The palette centers on creamy and sweet tones—a soft red and light beige that evoke comfort and coziness. These colors are reminiscent of warm milk, gentle sunlight, and the inviting atmosphere of a perfect café morning. The restrained palette ensures the brand feels sophisticated while remaining approachable.

03 / Applications

Brand In Action

The brand identity creates a cohesive experience across various touchpoints, from merchandise and packaging to digital applications, all unified by the warm, minimalist aesthetic and charming mascot system.

Physical Applications

The mascot coffee cups appear across merchandise including t-shirts, coffee mugs, and stickers, creating collectible items that customers want to own and share. Each application maintains the hand-drawn quality and warm color palette, ensuring brand consistency while allowing for playful variations.

The packaging design uses clean layouts with the mascot characters as focal points, creating an unboxing experience that feels like receiving a gift from a caring friend. The minimalist approach ensures the products feel premium while the mascots add personality and warmth.

Digital Presence

The digital applications maintain the same warm, minimalist aesthetic while showcasing the mascot characters in interactive ways. The clean layouts and soft color palette create a calming digital experience that mirrors the comfort of the physical café space.

04 / Process

Design Journey

This project was developed as a creative exercise to explore brand design principles while diving deep into Korean café culture and its unique aesthetic values.

01

Cultural Research

Extensive study of Korean café culture, understanding the emphasis on comfort, community, and attention to detail that makes these spaces special.

02

Concept & Mascot Development

Creating the three coffee cup mascots with distinct personalities, hand-drawing each character to maintain authenticity and warmth.

03

Visual System Design

Developing the minimalist aesthetic, color palette, and hand-drawn logo that would work across all brand touchpoints.

04

Application & Testing

Applying the brand to various products and materials, ensuring the system works effectively across different scales and contexts.

The design process focused on balancing minimalism with personality, ensuring the brand felt both sophisticated and approachable. The hand-drawn elements required multiple iterations to achieve the right level of "wonkiness" that felt intentional rather than sloppy.

This project served as an excellent exercise in developing a comprehensive brand system from cultural research through final application, strengthening creative problem-solving skills and understanding of brand consistency across multiple touchpoints.
