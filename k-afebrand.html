K-AFE: Redefining Contemporary Café Culture

A bold brand identity that merges Korean aesthetics with modern coffee culture, challenging conventional café design through raw typography and industrial textures.

Adobe Photoshop Adobe Illustrator
01 / Overview

The Challenge

In a saturated market dominated by minimalist third-wave coffee aesthetics, K-AFE sought to establish a distinctive brand identity that would stand out while remaining authentic to its Korean-influenced roots.

The project required developing a comprehensive visual system that could work across multiple touchpoints—from physical café spaces to digital platforms—while maintaining consistency and impact. The challenge was to create something that felt both familiar and completely unexpected.

Our approach centered on embracing imperfection and celebrating the raw, unfiltered aspects of café culture that are often polished away in contemporary branding. Instead of following trends, we chose to create our own visual language.

"Beauty is in the eye of the beholder, but what is beauty though? It's subjective."
K-AFE Brand Philosophy
02 / Visual Identity

Brand DNA

The visual identity rejects standardized café aesthetics in favor of a brutalist approach that celebrates imperfection and authenticity.

Typography System

The typeface selection combines stark, industrial sans-serifs with subtle Korean typographic influences. Headlines use condensed weights with negative letter-spacing to create tension and impact, while body text maintains readability through careful spacing and hierarchy.

Color Philosophy

The color palette is deliberately constrained—primarily black and white with strategic use of neon pink (#ff00d4) as an accent. This limitation forces creativity within constraints and ensures maximum impact when color is employed.

03 / Applications

Brand In Action

The brand identity extends across multiple touchpoints, from packaging and merchandise to digital platforms and environmental design.

Physical Applications

Coffee cups, merchandise, and packaging all feature the distinctive K-AFE aesthetic. The brutalist approach translates effectively to physical objects, creating memorable branded experiences that customers want to share and collect.

Signage and environmental graphics use industrial materials and bold typography to create immersive brand experiences. The café space itself becomes part of the brand narrative, with raw concrete, exposed steel, and neon accents.

Digital Presence

The website and social media presence maintain the same bold aesthetic while ensuring usability and accessibility. Interactive elements use the brand's signature glitch effects and bold typography to create engaging digital experiences.

04

Implementation

Rollout across physical and digital platforms, with ongoing refinement based on real-world application feedback.

The iterative process involved constant testing and refinement. Early concepts were too aggressive and risked alienating potential customers, while later iterations found the right balance between bold and approachable.

Client collaboration was crucial throughout the process. Regular feedback sessions ensured the final identity aligned with business objectives while pushing creative boundaries.
