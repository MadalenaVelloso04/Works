K-AFE Brand Identity

A contemporary café brand identity inspired by the cultural fusion of Korean aesthetics and coffee culture. Bold typography meets minimalist design to create a unique brand experience that stands out in the competitive café marketplace.

ESTABLISHED 2023

BEAUTY IS IN THE EYE OF THE BEHOLDER.

K-AFE's visual identity challenges conventional café aesthetics by embracing bold contrasts and unexpected design elements. The brand's approach to beauty is deliberate and unapologetic, creating spaces and experiences that demand attention rather than seeking approval.

WHAT IS BEAUTY THOUGH? IT'S SUBJECTIVE.

K-AFE rejects the standardized aesthetic of third-wave coffee shops. Instead, it embraces a raw, unfiltered design language that celebrates imperfection. Through stark typography, unconventional layouts, and industrial textures, the brand creates a memorable and distinctive identity in a saturated market.
