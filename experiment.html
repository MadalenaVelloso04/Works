Timeless Morris

A tribute to authentic creation in the age of artificial intelligence.

Timeless Morris is a magazine cover concept that pays tribute to William Morris and the Arts and Crafts movement. It highlights the need to return to hands-on, authentic creation in response to the growing influence of artificial intelligence in the art world. By echoing Morris's values, it calls attention to how AI is impacting real artists and reminds us of the enduring value of human craft.

02 / NR_02

Machine Mirage

The elusive infiltration of artificial intelligence into human creativity.

Machine Mirage follows the same visual language and thematic direction as my previous Timeless Morris project. This time, the message is more direct: a commentary on how AI is distorting our minds and creative processes. The concept is illustrated figuratively, showing multiple brains being infiltrated and corrupted by a strange, intangible force — representing the elusive and invasive nature of artificial intelligence.

Heritage

Everyday objects that connect us to home.

Heritage is a three-poster project where I explored the idea of everyday objects I can't live without. To add depth, I connected each item to my sense of home, a personal reflection shaped by my experience as an Erasmus student.

Material Memories

A new visual perspective on familiar connections.

Material Memories is the second poster in my three-part project exploring everyday elements that connect me to home. Continuing the same theme, I shifted the graphic style and featured different objects to express the idea from a new visual perspective.

Primavera

The spirit of Portuguese festivals captured in mixed media.

Primavera is an experimental poster where I mixed different hand-drawn sketches I made previously. I was inspired by some of my favorite Portuguese festivals and how they make me feel, portraying that as visually as I could.

Bitmap Project

Climate change through bold, screen-printed visuals.

Bitmap Project is a series of posters focused on exploring overlays using only bitmap textures, all designed with silkscreen printing in mind. Using a consistent color palette, I approached the theme of climate change through bold, graphic visuals tailored for the screen printing process.

Encapsulate Culture

Preserving Portuguese culture through 3D metaphor.

Encapsulate Culture is the final piece in my daily objects project, created using mixed media including Blender 3D modeling and Photoshop. The concept was to literally encapsulate Portuguese culture and preserve it while living abroad. I represented this through a modeled moka pot — symbolizing Portugal's deep-rooted coffee culture — enclosed in a plastic bag, as a metaphor for protection and preservation.

Mon Oncle Project

Celebrating Hulot's quiet rebellion through minimalist design.

For the Mon Oncle posters, I centered the design around Monsieur Hulot and his signature items—hat, pipe, raincoat, and stance—to reflect his quirky routine and warmth in contrast to the sterile modern world. Inspired by Bauhaus minimalism, I used clean compositions, warm textures, and a cheerful color palette to highlight the charm and gentle humor of Tati's film. The posters aim to celebrate Hulot's quiet rebellion and individuality through simplicity and thoughtful visual contrast.

MONSTERS Zine

The giants of the design world

"The giants of the design world", rare creatures capable of the new and the extraordinary, all and each one of them masters of creation, inspired these pages. Each fanzine is a unique view to honour and celebrate their work and legacy as well as an experimental piece of creation itself." The Monsters project was born during Global Design professional intensive weeks, where, over two immersive weeks, we engaged in a real-world project. This experience allowed us to develop and enhance our communication skills while deepening our understanding of what it means to work with a client—creating within guidelines, listening attentively, and aligning with clients' goals and visions.
