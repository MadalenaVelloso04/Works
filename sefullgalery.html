Sustainablearth: Environmental Consultancy Brand Identity

A comprehensive branding project for an environmental consultancy committed to assessing and managing environmental impact, with a focus on supporting small and medium-sized businesses in Africa.
Second semester of university, 2022

Adobe Illustrator Adobe InDesign Brand Identity
01 / Overview

The Challenge

Sustainablearth is an environmental consultancy committed to assessing and managing the environmental impact of human activities, with a particular focus on supporting small and medium-sized businesses in Africa.

The organization's mission revolves around promoting responsible development through ecosystem restoration, risk management, and environmental education. With values deeply rooted in sustainability, Sustainablearth empowers individuals and businesses to embrace sustainable practices through pragmatic, actionable solutions and an inclusive, transparent approach.

This was our first time creating a full visual identity for an actual client, and doing it in university made it even more exciting. We got to take what we'd learned in class and apply it to a real-world project that's all about positive environmental impact.

"Promoting responsible development through ecosystem restoration, risk management, and environmental education."
Sustainablearth Mission
02 / Visual Identity

Concept and Creation of the Brand

The brand identity of Sustainablearth emphasizes a clean and natural aesthetic, inspired by elements like rivers and leaves to represent the harmony between human activity and the environment.

Design Philosophy

The color palette, grounded in earthy tones and greens, evokes a sense of calm and connection to nature. Using simple and elegant typography, the brand communicates with a positive and empowering tone, positioning itself as a trusted, informative resource.

Logo Design

The logo, combining the leaf and river imagery, reflects the consultancy's commitment to environmental stewardship and sustainable innovation. The design balances professionalism with natural elements, creating a memorable and meaningful brand mark.

03 / Applications

Concept and Creation of the Applications

Sustainablearth's branding extends into practical applications, including business cards, posters, stationery, and other branded materials.

Each of these items carries the same simplicity and elegance seen in the core brand identity, ensuring that every interaction with the brand reinforces its values and vision. The posters, for example, are designed to be visually impactful yet uncluttered, aiming to capture attention and communicate the brand's commitment to a greener future.

Brand Applications

The comprehensive brand system includes business cards, promotional materials, branded merchandise, and digital assets. Each application maintains consistency while adapting to different contexts and requirements.

04 / Process

Design Journey

Working on Sustainablearth was such a great experience for our team. It was our first time creating a full visual identity for an actual client, and doing it in university made it even more exciting.

We got to take what we'd learned in class and apply it to a real-world project that's all about positive environmental impact. It was a rewarding way to dive into the world of brand design and collaboration!

01

Research & Discovery

Understanding the environmental consultancy landscape, client needs, and the specific challenges facing small businesses in Africa. This phase involved extensive research into sustainability practices and brand positioning.

02

Concept Development

Exploring natural elements like rivers and leaves as core brand symbols. Testing various combinations of earth tones and greens to create a palette that evokes trust and environmental connection.

03

Visual System

Creating comprehensive brand guidelines, logo variations, and application standards. Ensuring consistency across all touchpoints while maintaining flexibility for different contexts.

04

Implementation

Developing practical applications including business cards, posters, stationery, and branded merchandise. Each piece reinforces the brand's commitment to sustainability and professionalism.

The project challenged us to balance environmental messaging with professional credibility. We learned the importance of creating a brand that speaks to both emotional and rational decision-making, particularly in the B2B environmental services sector.

This university project provided valuable real-world experience in client collaboration, brand strategy, and comprehensive visual identity development. The positive environmental impact focus made it especially meaningful for our team.
