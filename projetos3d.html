01 / Gare do Oriente Model

Gare do Oriente SketchUp 3D Model

Calatrava's intricate architecture modeled

Gare do Oriente As part of the project of total rebrand of the transportation hub of Gare do Oriente in lisbon, we had to also model the main floors and their new wayfinding that we created. We used Sketchup as the main tool to model each section, and to give it its final enhancement we rendered the images in Enscape.

ECO Exhibition

ECO Exhibition brought to life.

ECO Exhibition was a museographic project made from scratch, from concept to visuals. I was in charge of bringing the concept to life, as a complete modeled version of the space and its artworks, which i modeled following the specific measurements of the originals. It was fully modeled in blender.
