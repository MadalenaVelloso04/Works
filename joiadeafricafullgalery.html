Jóia de África

Product Design for African Cuisine

A university project in collaboration with the Aga Khan Foundation focused on strategic rebranding and product development for a Lisbon-based African restaurant. Creating a portable working station that celebrates authentic African culture and cuisine while supporting small business operations through thoughtful design and cultural authenticity.

Challenge
How do you create a functional product that embodies the spirit of African cuisine while supporting a small business? The challenge was designing for mobility, organization, and cultural authenticity while ensuring the product could serve as both a practical tool and a celebration of African heritage.
Solution
A complete brand identity system paired with a portable chef's station featuring three removable drawers for optimal organization. The hand-crafted prototype includes custom silk-screen fabric design that reflects African cultural patterns and colors, creating a mobile workspace that brings authentic cuisine anywhere.
Collaboration
Developed in partnership with the Aga Khan Foundation, ensuring cultural sensitivity and authentic representation of African culinary traditions in the design process.

Technical Specifications
The portable station measures 60cm x 40cm x 35cm when closed, optimized for easy transport while maximizing internal storage capacity. The modular drawer system features three distinct compartments: a spice drawer with 12 individual containers, a utensil drawer with custom-fitted organization, and a preparation drawer with removable cutting board and ingredient containers.

Construction utilizes sustainable birch plywood with precision laser-cut joints for durability and ease of assembly. The fabric elements are printed on organic cotton using traditional silk-screen techniques with custom-mixed heritage colors that reflect authentic African palette traditions.

All hardware components are selected for longevity and smooth operation, with soft-close drawer mechanisms and robust corner reinforcements. The design incorporates ventilation systems for spice storage and moisture management to maintain ingredient freshness during transport.
Impact & Applications
The design solution addresses multiple operational challenges faced by small African restaurants and food service operations. Primary applications include mobile food services, catering operations, cooking demonstrations, cultural events, and temporary restaurant setups.

Beyond immediate functional benefits, the project serves as a model for culturally sensitive design practices, demonstrating how traditional craftsmanship can be integrated with contemporary product design. The comprehensive brand system provides a foundation for expanding cultural food businesses while maintaining authentic identity.

The project has potential for adaptation to other cultural cuisines and mobile food service applications, with the modular design allowing customization for different culinary traditions and operational requirements.
